Daniel Carter grew up on Chicago's southwest side, the son of a machinist and a public
school teacher. He was the first in his family to attend law school, and he still
remembers what it felt like to sit across from an attorney who talked over his head
and rushed him out the door. That experience shaped how he built his own practice.

After graduating from the University of Illinois College of Law in 2005, Daniel spent
six years at a mid-sized downtown litigation firm, where he first-chaired more than
forty trials ranging from auto collisions to contract disputes. In 2011, he left to
start Carter Law Group — a practice built around a simple premise: clients should
always be able to reach the lawyer actually working on their file.

Today, Daniel keeps his caseload intentionally limited. He believes that the quality
of representation drops the moment an attorney is spread too thin, and he would
rather turn away a case than hand it off to someone the client has never met. Outside
the office, he coaches youth basketball in his old neighborhood and serves on the
volunteer board of a Cook County legal aid clinic.
